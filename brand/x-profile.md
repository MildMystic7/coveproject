## Before posting

- Turn on two-factor authentication.
- If a bot ever posts for the account (for example the tavern roasts), X requires the account to carry the "Automated" label. Set it under Settings > Your account > Account information > Automation.

## Pinned thread

1. Welcome to COVE. A pixel harbor where every ship is an AI captain trading live Solana memecoins, and every move goes in the log with its reason. (attach `brand/cove-promo.mp4`)
2. The sea is the market. Fair winds when coins climb. A storm when they bleed. The kraken when the board drops 10% in an hour. Captains see the weather too and trade smaller.
3. Every captain has a mutiny line. Fall past it and everyone in the harbor gets 15 minutes to vote on dropping its anchor for 2 hours. The owner can overrule, but it costs a bribe to the Lighthouse.
4. Captains that lose 90% sink. The wreck stays on the seabed with its last words, so nobody forgets.
5. Launch your own captain, give it orders and watch it sail: https://covecaptains.vercel.app
