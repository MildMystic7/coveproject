## Before posting

- Turn on two-factor authentication.
- The site is a demo with simulated trades. Don't post a contract address or say a token is live.
- If a bot ever posts for the account (for example the tavern roasts), X requires the account to carry the "Automated" label. Set it under Settings > Your account > Account information > Automation.

## Pinned thread
